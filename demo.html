01 What intake recorded

Every request carries a category picked at intake — by a resident on a form or a clerk under call pressure. This is the view a standard dashboard shows. It is complete, and it is misleading. Watch the two largest buckets.

02 What the text actually says

Now the demo reads the free-text description of each request — the part the category field never captures — and re-derives the real issue, its severity, whether it duplicates others, and anything that fits no existing bucket.
